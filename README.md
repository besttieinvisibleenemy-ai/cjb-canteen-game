# CJB Staff Canteen Game

A single-file HTML5 time-management game about running the staff canteen at Coimbatore Airport (CJB). Open the GitHub Pages site to play in a browser.

The main page (index.html) is version 11. It adds:

- shift pacing: each of the 4 shifts (morning tiffin, lunch, snacks, dinner) lasts at least 1, 1.5 or 2 real hours in live play (⏱ Shift pace). A "Fast test" switch removes the limit; Run simulation and ⏭ Day stay instant
- the instructor is now Seema (old saves with the name Jarvis are renamed). Her commentary goes through one queue, so lines never overlap each other, your typing, your voice-over or the explainer. A female voice is preferred where the phone has one
- background music: Mozart, Symphony No. 40 in G minor, K. 550 (movements I and IV), with an on/off switch, volume and a credits panel. The music goes quieter while Seema speaks
- refuel aptitude (pipes and cisterns): the Cessna VT-CJS on stand 2 has left, right and centre tanks. Every hour Bowser 2 makes 3 fill problems (two pipes together, a pipe with a return leak, pipes one after another) and 1 reverse problem (defuel after extra fuel was loaded). Multiple choice, timer, step-by-step solutions, and the answers match the times the simulation logged
- Seema explains the day after it ends: fuel, seating, trains, boats and profit/loss, with the camera flying to each place, animated diagrams and narration
- video recording: record Seema's explainer or the whole run (saved one file per shift) as WebM, or MP4 where the browser supports it

Version 10 (8-seat rest room and seating reasoning) is kept as v10.html, version 9 as v9.html, version 8 as v8.html, version 7 as v7.html and version 6 as v6.html. All figures are EXAMPLE values.
